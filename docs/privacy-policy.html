HUNGRY SPOT

Privacy policy

Effective 29 August 2026

Hungry Spot uses only the information needed to provide account, ordering, delivery, and payment features.

Information we process

  • Account details such as name, email address, phone number, and Firebase user ID.
  • Your phone number is sent to and stored by Google Firebase to deliver verification SMS messages and help prevent spam and abuse. Hungry Spot does not generate or store the one-time verification code.
  • Delivery or pickup selections, saved addresses, favorites, cart state, and order history.
  • Approximate or precise location only when you choose location-based address features and grant device permission.
  • Payment status, amount, currency, and Stripe payment reference. Hungry Spot does not store full card numbers or security codes.
  • Basic technical information required by Firebase, Stripe, and the operating system to secure and operate the app.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate customers, prepare and deliver orders, save customer choices, process or verify payments, prevent abuse, provide support, and meet legal obligations.

Service providers

Hungry Spot uses Google Firebase for authentication (including phone-number verification and SMS abuse prevention), database, and server functions; Google location services for optional address tools; and Stripe for card payment processing. Those providers process data under their own privacy terms.

Retention and security

Access to customer records is restricted by authenticated ownership rules. Data is kept only while needed for the services above, account support, fraud prevention, or applicable legal requirements.

Your choices

You can edit profile and address information in the app, deny location permission, or delete your account from Profile → Privacy & account. See the account deletion instructions.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the developer support contact displayed on Hungry Spot’s Google Play listing. Never include card numbers, passwords, or other secrets in a support message.
