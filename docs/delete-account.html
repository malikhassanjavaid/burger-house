HUNGRY SPOT

Delete your account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete inside the app

  1. Sign in to Hungry Spot.
  2. Open Profile, then Privacy & account.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Request deletion without the app

Open the Hungry Spot Google Play listing and use its developer support contact. State that you want the Hungry Spot account connected to your email deleted. Support may ask you to verify account ownership; never send a password or card number.

What is deleted

Your Firebase Authentication account, Hungry Spot profile, saved details, order records, and payment-verification references connected to your user ID are deleted. Information that must be retained for fraud prevention, dispute handling, tax, or other legal obligations may be kept only for the required period.
